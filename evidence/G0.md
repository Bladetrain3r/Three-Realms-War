## 3. What the evaluation caught that reading did not

Found by recomputing, fixed before the commit:

- A level-1 Shieldwarden's HP was written as 121; the formulas give **120** (VIG 22 x 1.1 floors to 24, x 5).
- The gear-scale sentence said plain item-level-50 gear adds "1.1x to 1.7x" of base and Heirloom "2x to 3x". The formulas
  give **1.0x to 2.3x** and **1.9x to 4.3x**. (The second is closer to Ziggy's "multiples of the base" than what was written.)
- One worked example's label assumed nine bonus-line kinds; the table has ten.
- One example line had prose where the checker expected the bare value.
- Three formulas had no worked example (lifesteal, the mend tick, salvage): added as WE-31 to WE-33.

## 4. A plausibility check on the curves (scratch, not a balance result)

`node evidence/G0-scratch-curves.mjs` (output in `evidence/G0-scratch-curves-output.txt`; fixed seed). A crude 4 v 4
auto-fight, no monster skills, no healing, no statuses. It reads as follows and as nothing more:

- Fight length is stable across levels (about 4 to 5 rounds with plain gear at levels 10 to 50), so the mitigation
  constant and the 10x level curve do not break at either end.
- Gear is decisive: at level 10 and above, a no-gear party loses every fight and a plain-geared party wins every one. The
  transition is narrow in this model, which has no monster skills. Monster profiles will need tuning at G3 (see the STOP-0 report).

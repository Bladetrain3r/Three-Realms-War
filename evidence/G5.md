## Why (measured, not guessed)

- A hero at level 20 with 3-star Runed gear wins a four-encounter level-20 delve 1.00 (Midgard), 0.93 (Asgard), 1.00 (Helheim). A site is five or six fights in a row (two per floor, the boss last) at level E + idiv(dist, 3) (dist 4 to 14 is +1 to +4 levels), with HP carried between floors, and the enemy curve is steep: the same party wins a level-22 delve 0.90, level-24 0.60, level-26 0.24. A wipe kills everyone, so the delve's attrition becomes a death rate.
- Asgard is the hardest realm for this party by a margin that already shows in delves (0.93 against 1.00).
- My first floor resolver healed only inside a floor; with healing after every won encounter (DESIGN 12.4 "between encounters as in delves") the wipe rate fell by about 0.05 to 0.1, not enough.

## 3. What was done to get here

1. **DESIGN 0.3** (permitted by 0.2): K 22 → 34 and monster profiles scaled. 116 → 124. `node checks/tune-enemy.mjs` (`evidence/G3-tuning.txt`).
   A search of about 770 settings found a ceiling of 22 to 23 of 29 delve checks: with hero base, gear and enemies all linear in level, no
   setting of those two knobs makes the better tiers decline with level.
2. **STOP-1** put four decisions to Ziggy with measured options (`evidence/G3-options.txt`).
3. **DESIGN 0.4** applied his answers: the enemy curve gained a quadratic term (K=30, Q=10, 41× at level 50); eight hero skill powers
   changed (six four-target skills ×0.7, Shield Bash and Cold Grip ×2); `matBase` 3; the three bounds above. 124 → 147.
4. The tank crit-immunity knob Ziggy offered was measured and not built: even permanent immunity moves tank class win rates by about 0.01.

## 4. Caveats

- The gear ladder carries no bonus lines (conservative: real gear is stronger); the mirror uses my own runbook per class
  (`checks/balance-runbooks.json`), so class results partly describe those runbooks. Neither is a threshold.
- One party (the four starting classes) for the delve tables; the mirror samples 36 of 495 possible teams.
- `checks/options.mjs` and `checks/tune-enemy.mjs` reproduce the STOP-1 measurements against commit `d7c4378` (they patch the old
  linear curve); to rerun them, use a worktree at that commit.

## 5. The one open check

`rime_mender + shieldwarden + skyrider + volva` (a healer, a tank, a ranged skirmisher and a hexer, three of them Helheim) wins **5.6%** of
its mirror fights against a floor of 10%. A diagnosis run did not find a harness flaw: the team loses 94 to 100% against strong and middling
teams, and replacing any single member with a Huscarl does not rescue it, so it is a team with little damage. It is a design question
(is a team this weak acceptable, or should Volva and Rime-Mender be stronger?), and loosening a floor is Ziggy's to allow. G3 closes when
that is answered and the check is green or the bound moved with his word.

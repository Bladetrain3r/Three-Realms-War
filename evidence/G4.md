## The four checks GATES.md names

1. **Every screen renders.** `client.test.mjs` opens the built page from `file://` at 1280 and 390 px, visits Hall, Hero, Forge, Delve,
   Settings and Battle, and asserts the heading, no console error and no horizontal scroll (the page was 402 px wide at 390 before the
   Equipment table was stacked on phones).
2. **Browser replay hash equals Node's.** The test imports a save made in Node (`newGame(content, 424242)`), presses Descend in the page,
   and compares the replay hash the page reports with `playDelve(...)` in Node: equal; the whole save after it is equal except `meta`; a
   second delve on the result is equal too. Also: `client/engine-check.js` replays a fixed delve on the device at every start
   (`Settings > About` shows it) and compares with a hash recorded in source.
3. **The runbook editor refuses invalid runbooks with the reason.** Five inputs are tried through the raw-JSON box: not JSON, a skill outside
   the hero's kit (`E07`), a threshold of 55 (`E05`: "integer from 10 to 90 in steps of 10"), nine rules (`E02`), a target on a brace (`E08`).
   Each shows "Refused" and the validator's wording, and the saved runbook is compared unchanged. A valid edit through the form saves and
   survives a page reload.
4. **Export then import restores the same save.** Byte for byte (canonical JSON), through the Settings screen, after a reset in between.
   A save with `level: 999` is refused with the key and reason; bad JSON is refused; neither changes the current save.

Also tested through the page: Forge attempt / undo / accept and unequip give exactly the save the rules layer gives; recruit, bench, add,
reorder and dismiss likewise; the Hall's disabled buttons carry the rule's reason as their title; an injured hero blocks Descend until the
"send anyway" box is ticked and then fights injured.

## Budgets (DESIGN 15), measured

`node checks/measure-client.mjs --write evidence/budgets/client.json`, `node checks/measure-art.mjs --write evidence/budgets/art.json`.

| Budget | Ceiling | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| Shipped bundle, uncompressed (`dist/`) | 400 KB (409,600 B) | **314,835 B** (game.js 304,130; style.css 10,252; index.html 453) |
| JS heap after every screen and a full replay | 256 MB | **4.6 MB** (`performance.memory.usedJSHeapSize`, precise-memory flag) |
| One battle frame at 1920 x 1080 (draw and raster, forced read-back) | p95 <= 16.7 ms | **median 4.4, p95 7.3, max 9.8 ms** over 300 frames |
| The page's own animation loop at 4x | 60 fps | rAF interval median 16.7, p95 16.7, max 16.8 ms over 120 frames |
| Save document | 512 KB | 12,068 B after a 160-action seeded bot game (8 heroes, 56 items); 38,157 B after 600 actions (24 heroes, 184 items); `tests/unit/save.test.mjs` asserts the ceiling. Measured by calling `canonical(save).length` on `tests/helpers/bot.mjs` play |

Art cost, cold cache (not a DESIGN budget, but the first thing a player waits for): hero sprite median 34 ms, monster median 42 ms (max
267), lord ~430 ms, backdrop ~330 ms, vellum sheet 1920 x 1080 150 ms; everything at once 5.9 s. A battle paints only what its replay needs
(about a second cold), one asset per timer tick so the page stays responsive, then caches.

Honest caveat on the frame number: my first version timed only the queueing of canvas calls (median 0.1 ms), which says nothing because the
raster happens later. The numbers above force a pixel read-back each frame. With the backdrop redrawn every frame the p95 was 13.8 to
18.2 ms (over budget on some runs); with the backdrop on its own canvas it is 7.3.

## What I looked at and what I think of it

- The vellum ground, woodcut frames and inked buttons read as the direction Ziggy gave. The figures are the weakest part: stylised,
  puppet-like volumes with convincing paint texture; they tell apart by silhouette and realm colour (red-brown Midgard, blue-grey Asgard,
  teal Helheim) but they are not "good art". The painter is a function of a list of shapes, so better figures are a matter of better part
  lists, not a different engine.
- The battle is a plain two-lane stage with stagger; hits shake, numbers float, downed units fade. Reduced motion (system setting or the
  Settings box) skips straight to the finished battle and the log.
- Not done in G4, by plan: the Expedition map and replay-file loading (G5, G6); sound; touch gestures beyond ordinary taps.

## Known gaps (also in STOP-2)

- After a wipe, injured heroes recover only by sitting out delves, so a party of four injured heroes can only recover by sending one forced
  hero for two delves. A cheap "rest" action may be wanted (design question).
- Pages must be enabled by the repository owner; the workflow is committed but I did not change any setting.
- Not tested: real GPU, Firefox, Safari, a phone. Chromium here is the only engine.

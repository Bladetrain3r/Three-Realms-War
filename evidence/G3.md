## 3. What changed under DESIGN 0.2, and what the search found

DESIGN 0.2 lets G3 tune the enemy curve constant and the monster profile tables, logged with the measurement. Result is DESIGN 0.3:
K 22 → 34 and the level-1 monster profiles scaled (VIG ×0.4, attack ×0.7, defence ×0.5). It moved 116 → 124 in bounds and opened
the gear gap (the baseline's 0.108 → 0.42).

Evidence of the search: `node checks/tune-enemy.mjs` (`evidence/G3-tuning.txt`): 144 settings scored on 29 delve checks at 150
delves per cell, the leaders confirmed at 900 per cell (the committed setting is tied for first at 23 of 29). Earlier, wider scratch
searches (288 and 480 settings, plus 6 uniform scalings confirmed at full size) found the same: **a ceiling of about 22 to 23 of
29 whatever the setting**, and a sharp cliff in win rate as K passes about 40. The reason is structural, not a tuning problem:
hero base stats, gear and enemies all scale linearly with level, so the *ratio* of hero to enemy power is nearly constant above
level 5 and each gear tier wins the same share at every level. Nothing these two knobs can do makes Fine, Runed and Heirloom
decline with level.

## 4. The options put to Ziggy (measured against the same committed thresholds)

`node checks/options.mjs` (`evidence/G3-options.txt`; about five minutes) applies each option to a scratch copy of the repository.

- **A. Add a quadratic term to the enemy curve** (one new constant): K=30, Q=10 puts **129 of 148** in bounds with the *current*
  profiles; the only delve check still out is level-50 Heirloom at 0.99 (bound ≤ 0.98). At level 50 the tiers read starter 0.08,
  Fine 0.47, Runed 0.76, Heirloom 0.99; at level 5 starter 0.92. The best purely linear setting reaches 124.
- **B. Hero and mechanic variants for the mirror** (53 checks; 17 out today): tank skills ×1.5 gives 11 out; hero AoE ×0.7 plus tank
  skills ×2 gives 10 out; a front-row damage reduction makes it worse (20 out); the mitigation constant is not the lever (12 → 4 gives
  18 out). My first hypothesis, that defence barely matters because the per-level mitigation constant swamps it, was tested and **refuted**.
- **C. Materials growth:** `matBase` 3 gives 6.3×; 4 gives 5.0×.

## 5. Caveats

- The gear ladder carries no bonus lines (conservative: real gear is stronger than the table); the mirror uses my own runbooks per
  class (`checks/balance-runbooks.json`), so class results are partly a statement about those runbooks. Neither is a threshold.
- Win rates are for one party (the four starting classes); the mirror samples 36 of the 495 possible four-class teams.
- The death proxy uses the design's 40% death save on heroes downed in won encounters; expeditions are not built yet.
- The scratch experiments behind the options (the quadratic curve and the mirror variants) patch a copy of `sim/`; nothing in the
  repository's `sim/` or `content/` implements an option.
